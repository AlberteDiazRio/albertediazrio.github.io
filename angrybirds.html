DESCRIPTION

Angry Birds Match is a colorful match-3 puzzle game where players join the Angry Birds characters on a fun-filled adventure across hundreds of exciting levels. Match tiles, create powerful combinations, and complete unique objectives to progress through vibrant worlds.

As players advance, they can unlock adorable Hatchlings, discover special boosters, and tackle increasingly challenging puzzles. With engaging gameplay, charming visuals, and regular events, Angry Birds Match offers a rewarding experience for puzzle fans of all ages.

My role in the project was as a Functionality QA Technician. I tested gameplay mechanics, UI flows, progression systems, and game features to ensure a polished player experience. My responsibilities included identifying, reproducing, and documenting defects while providing detailed reports to the development team.

I also performed regression testing, validated bug fixes, and verified that new features functioned correctly across different scenarios. Through close collaboration with developers and other team members, I contributed to maintaining a stable, high-quality experience for players.
