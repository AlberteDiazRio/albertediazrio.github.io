DESCRIPTION

Angry Birds Dream Blast is a colorful and engaging puzzle game where players help the Angry Birds through a variety of fun and challenging levels. By matching groups of same-colored bubbles, players can create powerful boosters, clear obstacles, and complete unique objectives across vibrant and playful environments.

As players progress, they can discover new challenges, unlock powerful combinations, and take part in special events and activities. With its accessible gameplay, charming visuals, and satisfying puzzle mechanics, Angry Birds Dream Blast offers a fun and rewarding experience for players of all ages.

My role in the project was as a Functionality QA Technician. I tested gameplay mechanics, level objectives, boosters, UI flows, progression systems, and different game features to ensure a polished and consistent player experience. My responsibilities included identifying, reproducing, and documenting defects while providing detailed reports to the development team.

I also performed regression testing, validated bug fixes, and verified new features across different gameplay scenarios and configurations. Through close collaboration with developers, designers, and other QA team members, I contributed to maintaining the stability, functionality, and overall quality of the game.
